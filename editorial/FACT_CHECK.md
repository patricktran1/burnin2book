# Fact Check — BURN IN, Version 2.0

Labels used throughout:

- **VERIFIED** — confirmed against an authoritative source cited in the notes.
- **AUTHOR-CONFIRMED** — supplied by the author in `source/FACTS_AUTHOR_CONFIRMED.md`.
- **AUTHOR MEMORY / VERIFY** — personal history drawn from the 2021 edition or reasonable inference; the author should confirm before print.
- **SUBJECTIVE EXPERIENCE** — the author's inner experience, presented as such in the text.
- **NEEDS SOURCE** — a claim the manuscript hedges or states generally because no authoritative source was available in the build environment.
- **REMOVE / UNSAFE TO ASSERT** — removed from the manuscript; recorded here so it is not reintroduced.

A note on verification method: the build environment had web search but direct page fetches to several government and journal domains were blocked by the network policy. For items marked VERIFIED below, the source was located and its wording confirmed from indexed excerpts of the cited page. **Before print, each VERIFIED item should be re-checked against the live page** (a fifteen-minute task with the URLs below).

## 4. Source-history conflicts that could not be reconciled from the supplied materials

1. **Timeline of Adrian's diagnosis.** The 2021 edition says Adrian was born in 2019 (house bought "in the fall of 2019 just after Adrian was born"), describes him as "almost two" and "not even two" at diagnosis, but also describes an October 2020 retreat as occurring after "the first few months of his illness." These cannot all be exact. Version 2.0 uses only "not yet two" and no dates.
2. **The earlier parking-garage thought.** The 2021 text places it in plastic-surgery residency without a year. V2 keeps it undated.
3. **The three-generation proverb.** The 2021 rendering was garbled; V2 uses the standard form (S17).
4. **The Ram Dass quotation.** The 2021 wording differs from the documentary line; V2 uses the documentary wording (S15).
5. **The DeWolf robbery.** Xbox vs. PlayStation 3 (S14); V2 says "a game console."
