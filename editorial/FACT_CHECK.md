Labels used throughout:

- **VERIFIED** — confirmed against an authoritative source cited in the notes.
- **AUTHOR-CONFIRMED** — supplied by the author in `source/FACTS_AUTHOR_CONFIRMED.md`.
- **AUTHOR MEMORY / VERIFY** — personal history drawn from the 2021 edition or reasonable inference; the author should confirm before print.
- **SUBJECTIVE EXPERIENCE** — the author's inner experience, presented as such in the text.
- **NEEDS SOURCE** — a claim the manuscript hedges or states generally because no authoritative source was available in the build environment.
- **REMOVE / UNSAFE TO ASSERT** — removed from the manuscript; recorded here so it is not reintroduced.

A note on verification method: the build environment had web search but direct page fetches to several government and journal domains were blocked by the network policy. For items marked VERIFIED below, the source was located and its wording confirmed from indexed excerpts of the cited page. **Before print, each VERIFIED item should be re-checked against the live page** (a fifteen-minute task with the URLs below).

### Correction made after the second pass

Chapter 6 originally quoted the label's **psoriatic arthritis** depression figures (1.0%, 10/998 vs 0.8%, 4/495) as though they were the pooled result for all controlled trials. They are not: the label reports depression incidence separately for each indication studied. Two problems followed. The figures were imprecise in a chapter whose whole argument is about precision, and quoting a single indication's numbers risked implying what the author took the drug for, which the manuscript deliberately withholds.

The passage now uses the pooled, indication-independent figures (0.2% serious depression, 0.3% discontinuation, 0.2% suicidal ideation and behavior, all of 1,441) and describes the depression incidence as "roughly one percent … with the exact figures differing by which condition was being treated."

The same review found a material omission. The label records that **two subjects taking placebo died by suicide during the trials while no subject taking apremilast did.** Leaving that out overstated the signal in the author's favour. It is now in the text, introduced as "the sentence I would have left out if I were building a case rather than describing one."
