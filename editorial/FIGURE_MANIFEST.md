# Figure Manifest — BURN IN, Version 2.0

All 36 visual assets extracted from the 2021 print PDF (`source/figures/`, page-numbered) were reviewed against the Version 2.0 argument. Retained figures were copied to `assets/figures/` (or `assets/cover/` for identity assets) under stable descriptive filenames; the original extracted filename is preserved here for audit. No original artwork was redrawn. Captions in the manuscript are the alt text of each Markdown image and were written for Version 2.0.

**Totals:** 36 reviewed · **KEEP 9** · **MODIFY CONTEXT 16** · **CUT 11**. Twenty-three figures appear in the manuscript body; two (the 2021 cover and title page) are retained as visual-identity references in `assets/cover/`.

Palette sampled from the originals for the web/print presentation: orange `#dc5231`, grays `#4a4d4e`, `#a4a6a7`, `#d0d3d3`, white paper.

## Notes for the designer

- The V2 cover (`assets/cover/burn-in-v2-cover.png` and `.svg`) is a typographic derivation of the original stacked wordmark in the original palette. It is a placeholder for a designer's final cover, not final art.
- Figure 04 (ruler) is very wide and short; the preview constrains figures to the reading measure.
- Figures 30 and 31 should sit consecutively as a pair.
- Every retained figure appears exactly once in the manuscript; `scripts/validate.mjs` warns on duplicates or unplaced retained figures.
