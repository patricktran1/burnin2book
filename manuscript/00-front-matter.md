## A Note to the Reader

This book describes a suicide attempt, mostly in Chapter 5. I have written about it without the details that could serve anyone as instruction, and I have tried not to make it either smaller or grander than it was. Some readers will want to know that before they start, and some will want to skip that chapter for now and come back to it. Both are fine. The book will still make sense.

If you are in crisis as you read this, put the book down. In the United States you can call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, at any hour. Elsewhere, use your local emergency number or crisis line. The book will be here when you get back. I would rather you were here to read it.
