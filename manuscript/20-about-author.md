# About the Author

Patrick Tran, MD, is a dermatologist and fellowship-trained Mohs micrographic surgeon. He graduated summa cum laude from the University of California, Berkeley, with a degree in Molecular and Cell Biology, and earned his medical degree at the University of Michigan. He trained in plastic and reconstructive surgery before changing specialties to dermatology, and has published in peer-reviewed journals including *JAMA Dermatology* and *Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery*.

He is the author of *The Tran Hammer Method*, an intuitive approach to solving the Rubik's Cube, and of the first edition of *Burn-In* (2021). He is the father of Adrian and Elliot.
