## Evolution of the thesis

### Original thesis

Burnout comes from the outside. Burn in by changing your internal mindset, building financial freedom, and becoming less dependent on external circumstances.

### Version 2.0 thesis

External circumstances and internal state both matter.

Agency does not mean everything that happens to you is your fault. Agency means that whatever has happened, your next move still matters.

The goal is not to escape your life.

The goal is to **build a life you can stay in**.

Burn In means creating an inner operating system, relationships, financial margin, boundaries, habits, and external systems capable of containing ambition without allowing that ambition to consume the person generating it.
